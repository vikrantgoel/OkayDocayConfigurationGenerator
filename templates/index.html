🔒 Session-Based Configuration Management

Your configurations are automatically saved to your browser session. Here's what you need to know:

  • Auto-save: Your current work is automatically saved every 30 seconds
  • Session isolation: Your configurations are private to your session
  • Session duration: Sessions last for 2 hours of inactivity
  • Data persistence: Use "Save to Session" to permanently save configurations
  • Export: Download configurations as JSON files for external storage
